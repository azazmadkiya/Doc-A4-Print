Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

Doc A4 Print ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

1. Information Collection & Usage

  • Local Document Processing: Doc A4 Print processes your documents, ID cards (Aadhaar, PAN, voter cards, etc.), and photos 100% locally on your Android device.
  • No Cloud Uploads: We do not collect, store, upload, or share your personal documents or photos on any external servers or cloud storage. All cropping, filtering, and A4 layouts happen completely offline.
  • Camera & Storage: Required solely to capture photos of documents and select existing images for alignment and printing.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: Used to photograph ID cards and documents directly in the app.
  • Storage / Media: Used to load images from your gallery for cropping and saving exported A4 PDFs.

3. Data Security

  • Because your documents are processed locally and never transmitted to servers, your personal data remains private, secure, and under your absolute control on your device.
